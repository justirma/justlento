What is Lento?

Cuban Cafecito Culture

La Ventanita by Lento brings authentic Cuban cafecito to Charlotte. Using Panamanian beans, we're creating gathering spaces where the Spanish-speaking community can connect, slow down, and feel at home.

This isn't just coffee. Es comunidad. Es cultura. Es el futuro.

Coffee → Soil Transformation

We collect used coffee grounds from local Charlotte cafés every weekend and transform them into enriched soil over 2-4 weeks.

Waste becomes life. Your plants get the best soil. The planet gets a break. Todo gana.

Building toward something bigger

Lento is more than cafecito and soil. It's the foundation for a recreation center for the Spanish-speaking community in Charlotte— a place to gather, connect, and build together.

Every cup of coffee, every bag of soil, every connection made— they're all steps toward that 10-year vision. Slow, intentional, transformative.
